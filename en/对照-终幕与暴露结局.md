### [终-042] 暴露结局 · ascension(anchor_too_heavy)
【原文】
导师们最后一次调阅你的锚重曲线，摇了摇头：「太沉了。」
不是背叛，不是异端——只是数据说，你迁移不过去。你被永久移出名单，

【新文】
The mentors pull up your ballast curve one last time and shake their heads: “Too heavy.”
Not betrayal, not heresy—only that the data says you will not migrate across. You are permanently removed from the list,
and the file gives you a gentle word: resident. After that, at every launch, you look up from the ground
and watch the people lighter than you leave first.
