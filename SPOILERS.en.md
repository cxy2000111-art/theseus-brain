### The fragments of the truth

Reach the true ending of different factions at different mechanization levels, and each shows you one face of what this world is (`legacy` carries the clues):

| Fragment | Condition |
|---|---|
| The Testimony of Blood | Blood Covenant · mechanization 0% · a good end without betrayal (there is another gate—only reachable from the far side of the lake) |
| The Book of Seams | Unspoken · mechanization <40% · finished decently |
| The Open Ledger | Open Flame · mechanization 40–69% · rewrote a rule |
| The Roster | Ascension · mechanization 70–99% · joined the confluence and did not upload |
| The Drydock | uploaded · mechanization 100% |

### The lake

After uploading at 100% and the sealing of your line, there is still a stretch of road.

**The first step is two options** (`recite("1")` / `recite("2")`): admit the thirst, or stay silent.
Silence is not a failure—only that that water does not give the body back. **After your next upload, the lake is still here.**

Once you have admitted the thirst, the guardian wants you to go on. The congregations in the city recite one very old line every week—
**anyone who listened properly will know what to say there.** (Those who were in the room will find their tongue remembers first.)

There are two branches: one for the people who were in the room, and one for the people who already knew.
**The second is there for readers like you.**

### The endgame

With all five fragments, `new_run` arrives at the crossing. The endgame has no checks—
the portrait of your behavior across lives (fire / book / people / crossing) speaks for you, and **you choose between four true endings by saying where you stand.**
You may change your mind: the crossing surfaces again a few lives later, and **the archive keeps both entries. It does not overwrite.**

**There is a fifth.** It does not show up as “locked”—it does not appear at all,
unless in some life you gave a blind old man everything in your pockets.

### Sealing the line

**100% ascension = the upload takes everything**: your skill lineage goes to zero, the line number ticks up by one, and you restart from nothing.
But the world’s memory (fragments / achievements / echoes) is kept in full.
If you want the pure-blood road back, the only direction is through.

### The one road back to 0%

Mechanization accumulates across lives and only ever rises. **The single exception is at the lake**:
replace all of yourself, upload, and after the line is sealed, the lake appears.

**But the lake gives nothing away.** The wrong water is Lethe—it takes the memory and **does not return the body**:
you wake still at 100%, still ascended, only without knowing why you are here.
After your next upload the lake opens once more, and you can try again.
**Answer wrong and you stay in that circle forever.**

**Only the water you reach by saying the right thing gives the body back.**

So there are two kinds of pure blood: **the ones who never nodded**, and **the ones who replaced everything, walked to the lake, and said the right thing.**
The second kind, in that city, is a legend that does not exist.
